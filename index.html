ABOUT O’BARBA

Vintage souls. Modern craft. Everyday luxury, unisex.

Founded in Varanasi in 2018, O’Barba is a family salon built for real life — where chai is always on, consultations are honest, and the work is precise. Our rooms are dark, calm, and intentionally analog: warm wood, matte black steel, and brass that ages beautifully. We train in-house, we pay fair, and we never upsell what you don’t need.
