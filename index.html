— About O'Barba

Vintage souls. Modern craft.
Everyday luxury, unisex.

Founded in Varanasi in 2018, O'Barba is a family salon built for real life — where chai is always on, consultations are honest, and every chair feels like home. We blend the warmth of a neighbourhood parlour with the precision of a luxury studio: warm wood, matte black steel, and brass.

Ethos

Cruelty-free, vegan, low-waste. Every product we use is chosen with care for you and the planet.

People

70+ certified professionals trained at L'Oréal, Wella, and other global academies. Expertise you can trust.

Promise

Transparent pricing, zero hidden add-ons. What you see is what you pay — always.
